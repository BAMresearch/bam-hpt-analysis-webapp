Guideline Windows

Read-only check of the guideline clocks saved on Cycle Extract: the stored D / S, H, equilibrium and evaluation times beside the entry's stored Tsup, Q, P and COP and the same four on the evaluation window. After those means: H / eq / eval individual DB %, WB %, flow % and dTreturn % (Interval H bands on each saved window), D and S individual % (one number per interval on the union of saved spans), and eval ΔCOP (first against last slice of the saved evaluation window). After those, H / D / S mean deviations: one arithmetic mean per interval against its setpoint, in K (or % of set for flow) with the draft mean bands — a different quantity from the individual %, so H mean DB is ±0.3 K where H individual DB is ±1 K. Full-cycle mean Tsup, Tmean and Q stay on the Deviations page. Tsup cells are means in °C, not a sample share. Unsaved proposals do not appear here — save them on Cycle Extract first.

Every number on this page comes out of the database. The interval scores are computed once, when the clocks are saved on Cycle Extract, and stored beside them: no Plotdaten file is opened here, so loading is quick and leaving the page does not throw the table away. A row whose scores are not stored — clocks saved before this, or clocks that have changed since — keeps its clock times and shows n/a in the score cells, never a 0 and never an old percentage. Compute missing scores fills exactly those rows, file by file; it writes no clocks and never starts on its own.
